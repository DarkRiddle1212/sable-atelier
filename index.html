Our approach

Cut from intention.

We do not design for seasons. We design for the eleventh year — the point at which a garment has outlived every trend that greeted it.

Each piece begins as a paper pattern in our Porto atelier, is cut by hand from wool, linen or silk, and is finished by the same four people who have worked the room for a decade. Nothing is rushed. Nothing is marked down. When a cloth runs out, the piece retires.
